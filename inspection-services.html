Inspection Services We Provide

  • Initial Production Inspection (IPI) — at the start of production: raw materials, components and first output checked against your order
  • During Production Inspection (DUPRO) — mid-production visit to catch problems early, while they can still be corrected
  • Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI) / Final Random Inspection (FRI) — when goods are complete and packed: random sampling for workmanship, measurements, quantity, labelling and packing
  • Container Loading Supervision (CLS) — we witness the loading: right goods, right quantities, cartons handled and sealed correctly
  • Factory Audit — an on-the-ground assessment of a factory's production capacity, quality systems and working conditions before you place an order
  • Quoted per assignment — charges depend on the type of inspection, factory location and size of the order; contact us for a quotation for your shipment
