About Safe-T TECH International

An OEM/ODM clothing manufacturer in Sialkot, Pakistan — one partner for teamwear, fashion, uniforms and independent inspection services, built around low trial MOQs and honest, direct communication.

Who We Are

Safe-T TECH International manufactures clothing to order for clubs, boutiques, online labels and institutions, and exports to North America and Europe. Everything is made to the client's own design or tech file — never off-the-shelf stock — with private-label finishing throughout: we sew in your brand tags, and a "Made in [your country]" tag on request (otherwise our standard "Made in Pakistan" tag).

Our Divisions

  • Sports Teamwear — football, cricket, American football, basketball, baseball and more
  • Women's Fashion & Athleisure — seasonal collections, dresses, sets and activewear
  • Swimwear — private-label swimwear and crystal stage / competition bikinis
  • Bras & Lingerie — private-label intimates, lace sets, nightwear and bodysuits
  • Leather — leather jackets and outerwear, produced fully in-house
  • Streetwear & Outerwear — heavyweight hoodies, varsity jackets, tees and cargos
  • Uniforms & Protective Workwear — healthcare, fire service, industrial and institutional uniforms
  • Inspection Services — third-party inspection of buyers' orders at factories all over Pakistan

How We Work

  • Your design, exactly — send a tech file, design or even a photo; we manufacture it to your fabric quality, sizes and colours.
  • Exact quotation — prices are quoted against your actual design, never blind.
  • Low trial MOQs — 15–50 pieces per style (uniforms from 20 pcs/style; swimwear 30–50 pcs/style), so you can test before you scale.
  • Fast sampling — samples developed in 7–10 working days.
  • Checked before dispatch — in-line and final QC, with photos of the finished goods before shipping.

Our Founder

Ahmed Zahid

Founder & Director, Safe-T TECH International · Sialkot, Pakistan

Safe-T TECH International is led personally by its founder, Ahmed Zahid. Every order — from the first sample through production to final quality checking and dispatch — is overseen under his direction, so the person you talk to is the person responsible for your goods. His working principles are simple: low trial MOQs so small brands can start without a big risk, honest timelines that are kept, and quality checked piece by piece before anything leaves the factory.
